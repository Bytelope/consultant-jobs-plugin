# Privacy

The public plugin stores no credentials and does not implement the hosted
service. When connected, Claude sends the tool requests you approve to
Consultant.dev. Consultant.dev processes account information needed for OAuth,
search queries and filters, and operational service data. A CV is uploaded to
your Consultant.dev account only when you ask the tool to match assignments to
it. It replaces any CV already on file and is processed
by AI to extract skills and experience, after you have accepted that once on
Consultant.dev. You can remove it on Consultant.dev at any time.

The current policies governing the hosted service are:

- [Privacy policy](https://consultant.dev/privacy)
- [Terms of service](https://consultant.dev/terms)

You can disconnect the integration without deleting your Consultant.dev
account. See [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
